Accepted Image Types

This model is optimized for product/surface inspection style images. Use these rules before inference.

Best Inputs

  • Close-up product photos with clear surface details
  • Industrial or quality-check style framing
  • Good lighting and limited background clutter
  • JPG or PNG format

Poor Inputs

  • Screenshots of websites or app UIs
  • Selfies, documents, memes, text-heavy graphics
  • Tiny, blurry, or heavily compressed thumbnails
  • Multiple unrelated objects in one frame

Supported MVTec Categories

  • bottle
  • cable
  • capsule
  • carpet
  • grid
  • hazelnut
  • leather
  • metal_nut

Scoring Interpretation

  • 0.00 to 0.69 confidence: uncertain
  • 0.70 to 0.84 confidence: moderate signal
  • 0.85+ confidence: strong signal
  • Always pair with human QA decision

Production Rule Suggestion

  • If prediction is defective and confidence >= 0.85, auto-flag for urgent review.
  • If confidence < 0.70, route to manual review queue.
  • Log every prediction in operations dashboard.
