How this works

You upload one product photo. The model checks that image and gives a prediction: good or defective.

What to upload and what not to upload

Upload this

  • Single product photo (bottle, cable, surface part)
  • Object clearly visible and in focus
  • JPG or PNG with normal lighting
  • Close-up shots that show surface details

Do not upload this

  • Screenshots of websites or app UI
  • Memes, documents, posters, text-heavy images
  • Very blurry or very tiny images
  • Scenes with many unrelated objects

How to read results

  • Prediction tells model opinion: good or defective.
  • Confidence tells strength of that opinion.
  • Low confidence means uncertain.
  • Always make final decision with manual QA.

How to present this project

What to show in demo

  • Take/upload image on Home page
  • Live prediction + confidence + latency
  • How confidence changes for different images
  • How-it-works page with dataset examples

What to say clearly

  • This is a screening tool, not final QA authority.
  • Low confidence results are uncertain by design.
  • Manual review is required for final decisions.
  • The model performs best on similar dataset style images.
